About 520 commits, and nearly all of them landed in one place. The
OxideAV media framework took about 475 of them, spread across more
than fifty crates. The theme ran across codecs. Encoders were
measured against the standards' own buffer models and conformance
streams, and decoders were checked against official suites until
they matched, or until the spec itself turned out to be wrong.

## oxideav-mpeg12video: rate control by the book

[**oxideav-mpeg12video**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-mpeg12video)
(24 commits, the week's busiest repo) took its MPEG-1/MPEG-2
encoder from producing valid streams to producing streams that
are verified against the standard's decoder buffer model:

- an exact **Annex C VBV model** plus a whole-stream CBR verifier,
  then **CBR rate control** for MPEG-2 GOPs, MPEG-1 sequences and
  field-coded sequences;
- **MPEG-2 field pictures**: I/P/B field encode with a display-order
  field assembler, then frame pictures with per-macroblock
  frame/field prediction, field DCT and dual-prime, and adaptive
  16×8 motion compensation;
- **MPEG-1 D-pictures**, the DC-only picture type;
- runtime encoder registration, and a platform-deterministic
  cosine table for the DCT/IDCT.

The self-encoded corpus grew to seventeen streams, and the CBR and
field-coded additions were validated black-box against an external
decoder.

## oxideav-av1 and oxideav-avif: one decoder, two formats

[**oxideav-av1**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-av1) (19
commits) kept adding encoder "elections", where the encoder decides
per stream whether a tool pays for itself. This week covered
quantizer matrices, including per-segment levels; superres, with a
downscaled-width keyframe arm that was then extended across the
segmented, pyramid, temporal-ladder and spatial-SVC drivers and
paired with loop restoration at the upscaled extent; and
**film-grain** parameter estimation with chroma points and AR-tap
fitting. Each round pinned new streams in the conformance corpus,
taking it from 122 to 133.

[**oxideav-avif**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-avif) (18
commits) sits directly on top of it. Its first commit of the week
restored real pixel decode through oxideav-av1's registry decoder.
After that came the 10- and 12-bit paths end to end: primaries,
grids, alpha and AVIS image sequences, gain-map application, and a
pixel-to-AVIF encoder across the full depth and format matrix. An
external encoder's files now decode exactly at depth.

## oxideav-h266: conformance, and two errata in the spec

[**oxideav-h266**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-h266) (19
commits) moved from encoding its own streams to decoding the JVET
conformance suite, and the triage scorecard tracks the change. At
round 440 it read **0 pass / 8 fail / 26 undecodable / 22 error**.
By round 443 it read **1 / 14 / 33 / 8**: the desync family of
failures was gone, and `CodingToolsSets_A_2` became the first
stream to decode fully byte-exact. Along the way, the fixes covered
ISP and dual-tree CTB-128 transform units, CCLM neighbour bounds,
chroma deblocking QPs, multi-TB tiling, local dual tree (SCIPU),
and DMVR sub-block sizes.

Two of the commits are errata against the published V4
specification. The §8.7.4.3 LFNST matrices are printed transposed,
and the §8.7.4.5 32-point DST-VII/DCT-VIII sub-tables are the
top-right kernel block, not rows 16..31. Both surfaced during the
conformance triage.

## oxideav-fbx: blend shapes and constraints

[**oxideav-fbx**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-fbx) (16
commits) went deeper into scene semantics. It now has complete
blend shapes (static `DeformPercent`, multi-target morph animation,
one deformer per geometry), a full Constraint grammar round-trip,
the `InheritType` world-transform products, and a typed axis
convention enforced on decode. It also adopted the new
texture-reference and node-weight surfaces of oxideav-mesh3d
`0.0.5`, the shared 3D model it decodes into.

## Audio: official suites, old formats

The audio crates were mostly about conformance:

- [**oxideav-aac**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-aac) gained
  an ISO/IEC 14496-26 conformance harness (ER-LD, CCE, SBR-CRC) and
  a BSAC decoder: the arithmetic decoder, layer geometry and full
  block decode.
- [**oxideav-wma**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-wma) built
  its vendor-bitstream decode path end to end, from the superframe
  header through the VLC tables, band partitions and mid/side
  inverse to PCM. It is checked by a black-box SNR fit.
- [**oxideav-mp2**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-mp2) added
  the ISO/IEC 13818-3 multichannel extension and sweeps the
  official 13818-4 conformance streams.
- For trackers, [**oxideav-s3m**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-s3m)
  now plays AdLib instruments through its own two-operator OPL2 FM
  synthesis, and [**oxideav-mod**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-mod)
  reads SoundTracker 2.6 / IceTracker modules.

## Also this week

- **[oxideav-h264](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-h264)**: the
  PAFF/MBAFF co-located derivation is complete, and all four staged
  JVT conformance streams decode byte-exact. The encoder gained
  B-frame rate control.
- **[oxideav-vp9](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-vp9)**: lossy
  encode across the full format matrix (4:4:4, 4:2:2, 10/12-bit),
  chained P-frames as the default GOP path, and registry
  integration. **[oxideav-openexr](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-openexr)**
  added PIZ and DWAA/DWAB, both decode and encode.
  **[oxideav-svq](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-svq)** decodes
  SVQ3 I-frames at 299/300 pixel-exact.
- **[oxideav-embroidery](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-embroidery)**
  released `0.0.2`. It now decodes and encodes Husqvarna HUS/VIP in
  full over the ArchiveLib GL bitstream, and plugs into
  oxideav-core as a demuxer plus a vector decoder.
- **[purecrypto](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/purecrypto)**
  `v0.7.0` made much of the toolkit usable without an allocator:
  ML-DSA and SLH-DSA keygen, signing and verification,
  XMSS/XMSS^MT verification, single-tree LMS, RSA (PKCS#1 v1.5,
  PSS, OAEP), the fixed-curve EC primitives, and AEZ. It also
  closed constant-time gaps in secret-dependent paths.
- **[catcard](https://github.com/TibaneLabs/catcard)** got a chain
  registry with compile-time coin selection and an allocated USB
  identity (`0x39F2:0x0401`). Next to it,
  **[coldcard-firmware-archive](https://github.com/TibaneLabs/coldcard-firmware-archive)**
  is new: 124 officially-signed Coldcard `.dfu` images (Mk3/4/5 and
  Q1), verified against Coinkite's published SHA-256 manifest and
  kept for research and reproducibility.

## Next week

The OxideAV conformance work continues where the scorecards point:
more of the JVET suite for H.266 and more official streams for the
audio codecs. catcard has a USB identity now, and the next step is
a transport to go with it.
