A quieter week by count, and a very uniform one: nearly every
commit landed inside **oxideav**, the pure-Rust media framework, as
another set of its numbered work rounds (447 through 450) went
across the codec crates at once. No new repositories — instead, a
broad sweep of conformance work, where the measure of progress is
how many reference streams decode byte-exact. Outside the codecs,
**fstool** added two more filesystems and an in-browser editor.

## oxideav-h266: up the scorecard

[**oxideav-h266**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-h266) — the
pure-Rust H.266 / VVC decoder — had the most legible week, because
it keeps a scorecard against its conformance corpus (pass / fail /
unsupported / error). Three rounds of tranches moved it from
**1P/14F/33U/8E to 34P/12F/9U/1E**. Round 447 dissolved the affine
non-merge gate and made temporal motion, GPM and PROF/BDOF borders
spec-exact; round 449 got **every corpus intra picture byte-exact**
and landed SBT parsing, which made a whole family of parse-desync
errors disappear (29E → 2E); round 450 fixed MMVD offset derivation,
DMVR chroma bounding, CIIP gating and the spatial-merge scan — and
the "poc-1 luma inter" failure family collapsed with it.

## oxideav-opus: DTX and reduced-rate decode

[**oxideav-opus**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-opus) filled
in the parts of Opus that real streams lean on. **DTX**
(discontinuous transmission) now works on both sides — SILK
encoder suppression with the 400 ms refresh, CELT-only and hybrid
DTX, and zero-length frames held through concealment — with a
reference-stream fixture as a permanent gate. RFC 7845 §4.1 gap
repair composes PLC packets for missing ranges. The decoder learned
**reduced output rates** (8 / 12 / 16 / 24 kHz) across every path,
with a SILK resampler bit-exact against the reference, and the
codec now registers real decoder and encoder factories (with typed
encoder options) through oxideav-core.

## H.264 and H.265: wider encoders

The two older video codecs spent the week on their encoders.
[**oxideav-h264**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-h264) added
**monochrome 4:0:0** encoding (CAVLC and CABAC, I/P/B, the 8×8
transform) and **separate colour plane** coding on both sides — the
three-invocation decode of §8.1 and a per-plane encoder.
[**oxideav-h265**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-h265) gained
rate control: average-bitrate, pyramid-path, and VBV-constrained
(`bufsize`), plus spatial adaptive quantization — its first
`cu_qp_delta` writer — and a VUI frame-rate declaration.

## JPEG XL and MPEG-TS

[**oxideav-jpegxl**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-jpegxl)
reached a notable milestone: the ISO 18181-2 file format (typed
boxes, the `jbrd` bundle) and **byte-exact JPEG reconstruction** —
the JPEG XL feature that lets a recompressed JPEG be turned back
into the original file bit for bit, including subsampled transcodes.
[**oxideav-mpegts**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-mpegts) took
on North American broadcast: the full **ATSC PSIP** table family
(A/65 — STT, MGT, VCTs, RRT, EIT/ETT, DCCT) with Annex C Huffman
text decompression, CAT and TDT/TOT emission on the mux side, and
video T-STD pacing from the declared Rmax, under hostile-input and
fuzz batteries.

## fstool: littlefs, FAT12/16, and a browser editor

[**fstool**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/fstool) — the tool to
build, inspect, convert and repack disk images, filesystems and
archives — added **littlefs** (read, write and in-place edits of
lfs2 images, the filesystem common on microcontrollers) and
**FAT12 / FAT16** alongside FAT32, cross-validated against
dosfstools and mtools. A new in-memory authoring layer creates blank
filesystems and partitioned disks, and on top of it a **web build**
can create, edit and download images directly in the browser. ext
fixes covered journal revoke records and inline symlinks; releases
v0.4.21 and v0.4.22 shipped.

## Also this week

- **[oxideav-av1](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-av1)** — encoder
  work on S-frames (switch frames), film grain across formats,
  superres with multiple tiles, and error-resilient inter frames,
  taking its self-encoded corpus from 133 to 151 streams.
- **[oxideav-h263](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-h263)** —
  Annex V data-partitioned slices, Annex R independent segment
  decoding, UMV+ motion vectors, and a four-target fuzz harness that
  found an infinite loop and a quadratic re-decode.
- **[oxideav-ape](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-ape)** — the
  predictor cascade landed, so Monkey's Audio decodes whole files
  byte-exact on the fixture corpus; it's now wired into the registry.
- **[oxideav-vorbis](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-vorbis)**
  moved the IMDCT to an FFT decomposition (O(N log N));
  **[oxideav-mp2](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-mp2)** gained
  the §2.5 multichannel encoder; **[oxideav-wma](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-wma)**
  got lapped reconstruction and a registry entry;
  **[oxideav-basic](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-basic)**
  learned automatic WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE promotion.
- **[oxideav-png](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-png)** and
  **[oxideav-raster](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-raster)** —
  profiling rounds with byte-exact performance work (single-allocation
  PNG decode, cached sampling taps, O(1)-radius morphology).
- **[oxideav-nsf](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-nsf)** — a
  sub-instruction DMA engine with halt-cycle-exact DMC/OAM timing for
  the NES sound player's emulated CPU.
