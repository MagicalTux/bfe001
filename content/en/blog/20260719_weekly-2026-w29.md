After last week's 3,000 commits, this week came in at about 850.
The work went deeper into the projects that started recently.
**nixvm**, the syscall-level Linux sandbox, got Node.js running.
**graphitesql** made its full-text index write the same bytes
SQLite would. **z3rs** started replacing its batch pipeline with a
real DPLL(T) search. In the media stack, the AV1 encoder picked up
most of the remaining compound and screen-content tools, and the
ProRes codec was declared complete.

## nixvm: Node.js runs, and the sandbox gets stricter

[**nixvm**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/nixvm) runs a stock
Alpine userland with no guest kernel: the guest's syscalls trap
into a Rust kernel. Last week ended with a recorded limitation:
Node.js loaded but its event loop busy-spun. This week fixed that.
File-descriptor tables are now shared across threads, futex requeue
and timer wakeups landed, and the commit reads **"node runs"**.
Then it ran on the *software* x86-64 interpreter too. That took the
SSE operations V8's JIT emits (`PALIGNR`, `PTEST` and others),
syscall semantics that match the hardware (`RCX` ← `RIP`,
`R11` ← `RFLAGS`), and fixes to flags and decoding. The commit for
that step says **"TurboFan runs"**: V8's optimizing JIT generating
x86-64 code that nixvm then interprets.

The rest of the week tightened the sandbox:

- **W^X.** The interpreter now enforces NX on instruction fetch. The
  KVM backend builds guest page tables from the protection map, so
  a writable page can't also be executed.
- **Signals.** SIGSEGV, SIGILL and SIGBUS are delivered
  synchronously to guest handlers. KVM gained accurate page-fault
  handling and AVX state.
- **Scheduling.** Real time-based preemption on both backends.
  `KVM_RUN` runs lockless for true parallelism, and syscalls are now
  serviced in place under a big kernel lock instead of being handed
  off on every call.
- **Floating point.** A dependency-free soft-float with true 80-bit
  x87 and MXCSR directed rounding, plus the x87 transcendentals
  (`FSIN`, `FYL2X`, `FPREM`, …).

## graphitesql: byte-identical FTS5

[**graphitesql**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/graphitesql)
(82 commits, releases `v0.1.1` through `v0.1.3`) focused on a single
goal: making **FTS5 writes byte-identical** to what SQLite produces.
The commits show the investigation step by step. A divergence under
delete-heavy load was first characterized as data-sensitive, then
traced to missing automerge, then pinned to the trigger threshold
(`FTS5_WORK_UNIT=64`). Automerge scheduling was implemented. After
that came byte-identical results for delete/update inside
transactions, savepoints, prefix-index spanning segments, the
"double-cascade" crisis merge, and `detail=none`/`detail=column`
tombstones. An `integrity_check` now detects a malformed inverted
index.

FTS5 churn also exposed b-tree weaknesses, which were fixed. Page
splitting is now robust under large-key churn, siblings are
rebalanced on split, the reserved page bytes are respected, and
empty leaves are compacted after `INSERT OR REPLACE` and cascading
deletes. Further fixes ported SQLite's WAL checkpoint modes and
hot-journal recovery, added whole-file page accounting to the
integrity check, and added a process-kill crash-resilience suite
that runs on a schedule.

## z3rs: toward an online DPLL(T)

[**z3rs**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/z3rs) kept running its
differential sweep against Z3's regression corpus. Many commits
close a specific issue from that corpus, and several fix wrong
verdicts, two of them in the CHC engines. The structural change
is at the end of the week. The first **online DPLL(T)** pieces
landed: EUF conflict cores, Farkas conflict cores for arithmetic, a
verified equality-propagation mechanism, and a theory-propagation
hook inside the SAT search loop. This is the architecture Z3 itself
uses, where theories interact with the search instead of being
checked after it.

Alongside that: ports of Z3's `bv_rewriter` and `array_rewriter`
rules, complete uninterpreted-function interpretations in
`get-model`, nonlinear refutation by polynomial saturation, and a
parser that reports errors the way z3 does, recovers per command,
and supports `:print-success`.

## oxideav-av1: B-pyramids and screen content

The [**oxideav-av1**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-av1)
encoder, which became a real encoder last week, added most of the
remaining AV1 coding tools. Every new tool comes with a self-encoded
conformance stream:

- **Reference structure:** order hints, skip-mode, reference-frame
  motion vectors, and **B-pyramid GOPs** with backward references,
  out-of-order coding and `show_existing_frame`.
- **Compound prediction:** wedge and difference-weighted masked
  compound, distance-weighted (jnt-comp) compound, and smooth and
  wedge inter-intra blends.
- **Partitions:** the extended T-shapes and four-strip shapes, plus
  sub-8×8 inter and intra blocks.
- **Screen content:** luma and chroma palettes chosen by k-means
  clustering, and intra-block-copy search on keyframes.
- **Segmentation:** per-segment quantizers on P-frames.

The conformance corpus grew from 50 to 64 streams, and `v0.1.15`
was released.

## oxideav-prores: complete

[**oxideav-prores**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-prores),
the clean-room Apple ProRes codec, closed its remaining gaps in both
directions. That meant alpha-channel encoding, typed 4:2:2 and 4:4:4
alpha surfaces, 16-bit and deep (10/12/16-bit) alpha formats, and a
tighter rate-control search. Each piece was checked black-box against reference
encodes and decodes. The README dropped its status section ("the
codec is complete"), and the crate was released as **`v0.1.0`**.

## Also this week

- **[intlrs](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/intlrs)**, the
  pure-Rust ICU analog, added Umm al-Qura, Persian, Chinese and
  Korean Dangi calendars, Japanese-era formatting, `formatRange`,
  dictionary word segmentation for Thai, Khmer, Burmese and CJK, and
  pinyin, stroke and zhuyin collation for Chinese (`v0.5.1`).
- **[anydcode](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/anydcode)** added
  camera detection for Aztec, Micro QR, rMQR and MicroPDF417, 1D
  decoding at any rotation, and printed App Clip Code detection
  (`v0.1.2`).
- **[TibaneApp](https://github.com/TibaneLabs/TibaneApp)** became
  multilingual (English, French, Japanese, Portuguese), with a
  reworked Home screen and swap balances shown in USD.
  **[libwallet](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/libwallet)**'s Rust
  port reached request approval and object routing, and its web3
  provider is now restricted to same-origin frames.
