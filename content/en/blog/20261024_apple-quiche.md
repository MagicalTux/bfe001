Friends ask me for my apple pie recipe often enough that I've finally
written it down properly: the [apple quiche](../apple-quiche.html).

It's an apple pie, but the apples are baked in an egg and cream
*appareil* instead of just sitting on the crust, which is why we call
it a quiche. The page has the shortcrust (or where to cut corners if
you can buy one ready-made), how to slice the apples, the appareil,
and the two-step baking.

One warning repeated from the recipe: if you go with rum, use a
French-style amber *rhum agricole*, such as from Martinique. Other rums
don't work in this pie.
