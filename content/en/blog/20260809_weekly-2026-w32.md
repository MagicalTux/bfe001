The quietest week in a while, at about 300 commits, and a
detail-oriented one. The syscall-emulating sandbox spent it making
Linux behave the way programs expect, down to which `poll` bits a
closed pipe reports. The JavaScript engine went after the last
handful of test262 failures and the performance cliffs behind them.
The week-old Coldcard firmware went from scaffold to a Bitcoin
library with a screen, a keypad and a settings store.

## nixvm: the corners of Linux

[**nixvm**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/nixvm) runs a real Alpine
userland without a guest kernel. Guest syscalls trap out to a
"kernel" written in Rust, in the gVisor style. This week's 31
commits were almost all about faithfulness, the kind of behaviour
that tools only notice when it's wrong:

- **Signals.** `SIGPIPE` on writes to a pipe or socket with no
  reader, `rt_sigtimedwait` (so `sigwait`/`sigtimedwait` work),
  `sigqueue` delivery, a working `signalfd` (it had been a stub
  that never became readable), the signal mask in `ppoll` /
  `pselect6` / `epoll_pwait`, and signal deaths reported as
  `WIFSIGNALED` instead of `WIFEXITED`.
- **Time.** A real `nanosleep`, per-task CPU accounting that agrees
  across `clock_gettime`, `getrusage` and `times`, and
  `alarm`/`setitimer` delivering `SIGALRM`.
- **Processes.** POSIX process-group targeting for `kill`, the pid
  filter and child rusage in `wait4`/`waitid`, and
  `/proc/self/{exe,cwd,root,comm,cmdline,stat,status,fd}` reporting
  the live task. The CPU count now reports nixvm's own cores
  instead of leaking the host's through sysfs.
- **Files and IPC.** `O_APPEND`, `chmod` and `utimensat` (these had
  been no-ops), `FD_CLOEXEC`, `SEEK_DATA`/`SEEK_HOLE`,
  `EPOLLONESHOT`, non-blocking pipe reads returning `EAGAIN`, and
  `AF_UNIX` datagram sockets for syslog's `/dev/log`.

## kataan: 12 known failures

[**kataan**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/kataan) (30 commits)
went from 99.93% to **99.98% of test262: 51,878 of 51,890, 12
known failures**. A roadmap entry also records why each of the last
three failures "is not ours". The fixes went deep: program text is
now carried as WTF-8 so lone surrogates survive parsing (+9), and
`@@replace` concatenates WTF-8 canonically. Source-phase imports
also landed. Intl picked up per-calendar eras and month names,
`formatRange` routed through intl's range support, and `intl
0.6.1`'s `minimumGroupingDigits` (+6).

The other half of the week was performance. Many string and array
operations had a hidden O(n) per call. `slice`, `substring`,
`indexOf`, `at`, `push` and `delete` all had one, built-in
iterators copied their buffer on every step, and every property
access on a string materialized it. The fixes are rope-joined
`concat` and template literals, O(1) indexed reads with an ASCII
fast path, and in-place rope flattening. Each one is logged in the
roadmap as it closed.

## catcard: from scaffold to a Bitcoin stack

[**catcard**](https://github.com/TibaneLabs/catcard), the
clean-room Rust firmware for Coldcard hardware started last Sunday,
spent its first full week (17 commits) on the parts a wallet can't
do without, each as a separate host-testable crate:

- **Keys and encodings:** BIP-39 mnemonics and seed derivation,
  BIP-32 HD derivation over secp256k1, Base58Check, and Bech32 and
  Bech32m.
- **Addresses:** P2PKH, P2SH-P2WPKH, P2WPKH and P2TR.
- **Signing:** deterministic ECDSA and BIP-340 Schnorr, transaction
  parsing, and the BIP-143 sighash.
- **Hardware:** an SPI master, an SSD1306 display driver with an
  8×8 font, debounced keypad scanning with a self-test that echoes
  keys, an SPI-NOR flash driver, and an authenticated,
  power-fail-safe settings store on top of it.

One small commit says a lot about the approach: a panic handler
"that does not leave secrets in RAM". A hardware validation plan
was also written up, ahead of running any of this on a device.

## decryptd: a v5 kernel ABI

[**decryptd**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/decryptd), the GPU
worker for a distributed-compute project, shipped nine releases
(`0.1.31` → `0.1.39`). It picked up support for the v4 kernel ABI,
then replaced it with v5 and dropped every older one. The worker
now asks the compiled cubin what it is instead of reading a
manifest, takes its work space from the platform instead of the
job blob, and sizes launches to fit the output buffer. A run of
fixes followed the platform's axis-order convention, and one notes
that a brute-force box can exceed 2^128, so its size is no longer
computed.

## graphitesql: SQLite's function library

[**graphitesql**](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/graphitesql), the
pure-Rust SQLite (14 commits), filled in the extension functions
real databases use: `median` / `percentile` / `percentile_cont` /
`percentile_disc`, `base64()`, the `decimal` arbitrary-precision
functions, `regexp()` to back `X REGEXP Y`, `sha1()` and `sha3()`,
and the `ieee754` set. `sin`/`cos`/`tan` got Payne–Hanek range
reduction for large arguments. Multi-constraint `ON CONFLICT` now
resolves in sqlite's own check order, and the C API gained 64-bit
bind/result variants and zeroblob helpers.

## OxideAV: encoders close out

- [**oxideav-mpeg4video**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-mpeg4video)
  built its MPEG-4 Part 2 encoder in one sweep: a bit writer,
  forward DCT and quantisers, VLC emitters, an I-VOP encoder, then
  P-VOPs with motion estimation. A reference decoder reproduces its
  I+P streams bit-exact in a black-box cross-check. The DCT kernels
  were also made byte-deterministic across platforms.
- [**oxideav-opus**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-opus) landed
  the CELT pitch pre-filter (the inverse of the decoder's
  post-filter), which the README calls the end of the encoder arc.
  SILK-layer rate control, VBR arms and stereo Hybrid encoding came
  before it.
- [**oxideav-pixfmt**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-pixfmt)
  reached matrix closure: all 3,660 ordered conversion pairs across
  its 61-format universe now resolve.
- [**oxideav-mkv**](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-mkv) applies
  Matroska TrackOperation, joining blocks into virtual tracks and
  combining stereo-3D planes (RFC 9559 §18.8), with seeking on the
  virtual track.

## Also this week

- **[z3rs](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/z3rs)** closed more of
  its corpus: trig argument reduction by periodicity, π
  recognition, FP constant folding under any rounding mode, and a
  fix for a latent wrong-unsat on nonlinear closed-universal
  negations.
- **[fullrust](https://github.com/KarpelesLab/fullrust)** ported
  its libc-free toolchain overlay through every Rust release from
  1.90 to 1.95 in one week, and now self-tests its GitHub Action.
- **[oxideav-bitstream](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-bitstream)**
  gained a complete H.266 VPS walk with a byte-exact writer, plus a
  VP9 keyframe header writer.
- **[oxideav-embroidery](https://github.com/OxideAV/oxideav-embroidery)**
  widened to PHB, VIP, the plain-text `.col` companion and the
  Singer XXX header.

## Next week

nixvm keeps closing semantic gaps as real workloads find them, and

validation plan points at real hardware next. OxideAV keeps moving
